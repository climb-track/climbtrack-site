Climb Track

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026

Climb Track does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.

Information collected

None. The app makes no network requests. Everything you enter — logged climbs, saved workouts, clocked time, profile details — is stored only on your own device and never leaves it.

Camera and photos

Climb Track can use your camera, or a photo you choose from your library, so you can mark up a climbing route and replay the moves. That image is used on your device only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared, and the app does not analyse it on any server.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Climb Track does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
